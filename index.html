Rep ranges
Under the reps field the app names what that count usually trains: 1–5 strength, 6–15 size, 16–30 endurance, past 30 a very long set. The edges are soft. Heavy low reps are the best way to raise a one-rep max, not the only thing that builds strength. Muscle grows across roughly 5 to 30 reps — anything above about 30% of your max — provided the set is taken close to failure. Whether high reps are specifically better for endurance is less settled than the charts suggest. And high reps are not cardio: that is a different session, not a rep count.
How hard the set is matters more than which range it falls in — a hard set of 20 beats an easy set of 8. But one or two reps in reserve is close enough; going to failure every set is not required, and the edge it gives is small once total sets are equal.
